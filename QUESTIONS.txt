BIMBA — WEEKLY CHAT QUESTIONS

Three questions per week, starting October 4, 2026. The four-week cycle repeats.

WEEK 1
1. What’s your dinner plan?
Answer type: text box
Reply: That sounds like a lovely plan. I hope you enjoy every bite!

2. Anything fun today?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: I love how you find little moments of joy. You deserve them!
No: A quiet or hard day is okay too. You don’t have to make every day special. I’m glad you’re here.

3. Do you want to go to the theater with me?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: Great! I will buy tickets for us!
No: OK, I can find someone else!

WEEK 2
1. What’s one small thing you’re looking forward to?
Answer type: text box
Reply: I’m glad you shared that with me. Little things can give us something lovely to look forward to.

2. Did you get a little time to relax today?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: Good for you for making space to rest. You deserve a little breathing room.
No: You’ve made it this far today. Take a slow breath with me whenever you’re ready.

3. Would you like to take a little walk with me?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: Let’s go! Hold my hand and choose our direction.
No: That’s okay. We can stay right here and keep each other company.

WEEK 3
1. What song would you like us to listen to?
Answer type: text box
Reply: Thanks for sharing your pick. I like getting to know your little world.

2. Did something make you smile this week?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: Your smile is such a lovely thing. I’m happy you had that moment!
No: Some weeks are heavier than others. There’s no rush to feel better. I’m here for a little company.

3. Would you like to bake something together?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: Yes! Let’s imagine a kitchen full of warm cookies. You can choose the flavor!
No: No worries. Sharing a little toast sounds lovely too.

WEEK 4
1. What would make tomorrow a little better?
Answer type: text box
Reply: Thank you for telling me. I hope tomorrow has a little more room for what you need.

2. Are you proud of something you did this week?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: You deserve to notice your progress. I’m cheering for you!
No: Getting through the week counts too. You don’t need a big achievement to deserve kindness.

3. Would you like a cozy movie night with me?
Answer type: Yes / No
Yes: It’s a plan! Imagine a soft blanket, a good movie, and a little toast to share.
No: That’s okay. We can find another little adventure whenever you feel like it.
