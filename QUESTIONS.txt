Edit public/chat-content.js for all Chat words.
announcement + signature: your weekly note shown first.
questions: mood, hangout, and available date. Keep their IDs unchanged.
replies: the automatic companion replies and sending messages.
moodWords: positive and negative words used to choose the mood reply.
Change edition when editing questions, then republish the website.

Flow: announcement → mood → automatic reply → hangout.
Yes asks for an available date, then replies and asks for a name.
No replies gently and goes directly to the name.
Final Send saves all question answers with that name to the private inbox.
Replies are not recorded. Nothing is saved before the final Send.
Mail sent means saved to the private inbox; it does not send an email.

public/chat.js handles the steps and retry; public/chat-mood.js checks keywords.
GitHub push is separate and only happens when requested.
