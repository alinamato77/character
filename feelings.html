    About this little experiment +

    A guided, scripted Coach prototype for “Show How Sure It Is.” Every entry follows the same six steps: your words, your feelings, a little detail, a shared check, an editable draft, and a note to keep. The pixel faces are choices for you, not automatic emotion detection. Q uses your selected feelings and details to offer wording and reflection; it doesn’t use a live AI model. You can correct the interpretation and leave any feeling uncertain.
